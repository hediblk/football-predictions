How it works

Match model. A time-weighted Dixon–Coles goal model, blended with Elo ratings and expected-goals (xG) strengths, prices every remaining fixture.

Simulation. Each remaining scoreline is drawn 10,000 times. Tables are ranked with each competition's tie-break rules, so every percentage is a share of simulated seasons.

Ranges. Point ranges cover the middle 80% of simulated outcomes (10th–90th percentile). They reflect match randomness, not uncertainty in the team ratings themselves.
