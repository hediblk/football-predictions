How it works
Match model
A time-weighted Dixon–Coles goal model, blended with Elo ratings and expected-goals strengths, prices every remaining fixture.
Simulation
Every remaining scoreline is drawn 10,000 times and each table is ranked with its competition's tie-break rules. Every percentage is a share of those simulated seasons.
Ranges
Point ranges cover the middle 80% of outcomes (10th to 90th percentile). They show match-to-match luck, not doubt about the team ratings themselves.
