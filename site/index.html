How it works
Match model
A time-weighted Dixon–Coles goal model, blended with Elo ratings and expected-goals strengths, prices every remaining fixture.
Simulation
Every remaining scoreline is drawn 10,000 times and each table is ranked with its competition's tie-break rules. Every percentage is a share of those simulated seasons.
Ranges
Point ranges cover the middle 80% of outcomes (10th to 90th percentile). They show match-to-match luck, not doubt about the team ratings themselves.
Matches
Model record · this season

Every played match is forecast at 00:00 UTC on its match day from earlier results only, so the record never peeks at the result. A call is right when the model's likeliest outcome (home win, draw or away win) happens; the baseline always picks the home side.

How the ratings work
Rating
The share of the points a club would take off an average side on a neutral pitch, from the same match model that drives the forecasts: goals and expected goals, recent games counting more.
Attack, defence
Attack rates a club as if its defence were exactly as good as its attack, and defence the reverse, so all three numbers share one scale. Scores and Concedes are goals per game against an average side from the club's own league.
